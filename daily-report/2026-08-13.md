## Official Announcements

**[Auto mode is now the default in Claude Code](https://claude.com/blog/auto-mode-default-in-claude-code)** — Auto mode replaces manual per-tool approval with a classifier that only blocks actions deemed irreversible, destructive, or outside the project environment, rolling out to Pro/Max/Team on August 14, 2026 (Enterprise/API/Bedrock/Vertex/Foundry stay opt-in for now, broader rollout expected within the month). Anthropic's own study of 1,053 paid testers found the classifier flagged 89% of risky actions vs. 13.6% caught by humans, who approve ~97% of prompts without reading them — a strong argument for the switch, but also a reminder to keep an eye on what auto mode is approving on your behalf.
