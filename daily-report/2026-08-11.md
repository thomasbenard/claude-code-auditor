## Official Announcements

**[Anthropic is turning Claude Code's auto mode on by default](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/09/anthropic-is-turning-claude-codes-auto-mode-on-by-default/)** — Auto mode (executing actions without per-step approval, except irreversible/destructive/external ones) becomes default for Pro, Max, and Team accounts on August 14. Anthropic's data: in a study of 1,053 paid users, auto mode caught 89% of harmful actions vs. 13.6% via manual review, since users approve ~97% of prompts without scrutiny. Claude Code lead Boris Cherny: "The team and I use Auto mode exclusively... I couldn't imagine going back to permission prompts!" Additional safeguards include prompt-injection screening and customizable hard deny rules.
